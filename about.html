Wispr Stories turns your voice into shareable cards, in 44 languages, no account needed.

Every card supports 44 languages with automatic script detection. Speech uses two engines that pick the best one for your language. If yours isn’t listed, select “Native” for auto-detection. The app interface translates to 11 languages too, separate from speech.

Record from your browser’s microphone; the app captures your voice and transcribes it instantly. Attach the audio to your card: shared cards get a play button, downloads become an animated video with waveform. Free users get 15-second recordings (5/day). Pro unlocks 30-second recordings (50/day).

Share your card to any app, copy the link or image, or download as PNG. Links expire after 36 hours. Downloads are yours to keep.

Switch light or dark mode; your choice is saved. Install the app on your phone’s home screen. Cards auto-detect festivals and celebrations with themed images.

Frequently Asked Questions

Wispr Flow is an AI voice input layer that works across Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android. It lets you speak naturally and turns messy speech into polished text in any app: emails, documents, messages, code comments, and more. Wispr Stories is an independent project inspired by the same idea: making voice input fast and natural.

“Native” is for languages that the speech-to-text engines don’t specifically name but can still auto-detect. If your language isn’t listed individually, select Native and the engine will do its best with automatic detection. The card label will display “Native” instead of a specific language name.

Yes, completely free. No account or credit card needed. Free users get 15-second audio recordings (up to 5 per day) and 5 AI rewrites per tone per day.

Pro unlocks unlimited tone rewrites with no daily caps, 30-second audio recordings (up to 50 per day), and priority support. Upgrade directly from the app.

When you create a card, your text is sent to speech-to-text engines (Deepgram or OpenRouter Whisper) for transcription, and to an AI model (OpenRouter) for tone rewriting. We do not store your card text permanently on our servers. Only the metadata needed to display a shared card link (text, name, tone, palette) is stored temporarily alongside the card image.
