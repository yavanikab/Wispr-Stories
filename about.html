The story behind it

I wanted to send my mother a birthday wish — spoken, not typed. Something she could hear and keep. There was no simple way to wrap voice and words together in something that looked like a card worth receiving.

So I built Wispr Stories. It started as a small experiment. It grew because a lot of people wanted the same thing: a way to send their voice and their words together, in their own language, without an account or subscription.

Wispr Stories exists so the voices you love are never lost.

Cards created in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil — and 41 other languages. From a granddaughter sending birthday wishes to a grandmother receiving blessings in her mother tongue.

Why Wispr Stories

Think about the people who matter most to you. You have photos, maybe videos, old messages. But how often do you have their voice and their words together — in something you can keep? A birthday wish in someone’s voice, on a card. A short note from a parent, written and spoken. A memory captured before the moment passes. That kind of connection shouldn’t disappear. Wispr Stories was built to preserve it.

Questions

Wispr Flow is an AI voice input layer that works across Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android. It lets you speak naturally and turns messy speech into polished text in any app: emails, documents, messages, code comments, and more. Wispr Stories is an independent project inspired by the same idea: making voice input fast and natural.

“Native” is for languages that the speech-to-text engines don’t specifically name but can still auto-detect. If your language isn’t listed individually, select Native and the engine will do its best with automatic detection. The card label will display “Native” instead of a specific language name.

Yes, completely free. No account or credit card needed. Free users get 15-second audio recordings (up to 5 per day) and 5 AI rewrites per tone per day.

Pro unlocks unlimited tone rewrites with no daily caps, 30-second audio recordings (up to 50 per day), and priority support. Upgrade directly from the app.

When you create a card, your text is sent to speech-to-text and AI tone rewriting services. We do not store your card text permanently on our servers. Only the metadata needed to display a shared card link (text, name, tone, palette) is stored temporarily alongside the card image. See License & Terms in the footer menu for full details.
